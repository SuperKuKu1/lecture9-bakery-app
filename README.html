Home Bakery Orders

An app for a home baker who takes cupcake and cake orders by Instagram DM. It shows which orders can be taken without breaking the rules, and tracks every order from request to pickup.

1. How to use it

  1. Double-click Bakery-Tracker.html. No server or internet is needed. It starts on Monday 23 November with the orders that were already booked (Kim, Ross, Ali, Diaz, Fox, Ng, Brown, Silva, Wong, Chen, Haddad, Moreau).
  2. Press Fetch to read today's Instagram messages.
    • New orders that break no rule appear under New requests, with the pickup day's hours before and after. Press Reject on any you don't want.
    • Orders that break a rule appear under Incompatible orders with the reason.
    • Changes and cancellations appear in the Changes and cancellations box, marked Changed, Cancelled or Refused.
  3. If a day is full, press its +4 h $100 button to book a helper. That day's limit becomes 12 hours, and any incompatible order that now fits moves back to New requests. The button only shows for days at least 2 days away.
  4. Press Accept all and go to next day. All new requests are accepted, incompatible orders are rejected, changes and cancellations are applied, today's 5 PM pickups are marked as picked up, and the date moves on by one day.
  5. Repeat each day. Money: sales and helpers shows sales picked up, sales still to pick up, helper cost, and sales minus helpers. All orders lists every order with its status.

Everything is saved in the browser, so closing the page keeps your place. Start over from Monday 23 November resets everything.

Version 2: Bakery_Tracker_F.html

Same rules and same data, with a different screen built around the baker's two daily decisions: which orders to take, and whether a helper is worth $100.

  • Week strip on top: today plus the next 6 days. Each day shows a bar with accepted hours (solid) and today's requested hours (striped), its orders, and its helper button.
  • One message list: orders, changes and cancellations in the order they arrived, each with a clear verdict ("Can accept · $120", "Cannot accept", "Will cancel") and the reason.
  • Helper advice: when an order doesn't fit for lack of hours, the app works out what a helper on that day would let you take, for example "Book a helper on Sat 28: take Singh (+$160) for $100". One click books it.
  • Undo reject: a rejected request can be brought back before you go to the next day.
  • Money in the header: sales, helper cost and net are always visible.

The two versions save separately, so you can try both.

5. How the code is organised

  • Data: state.orders is the Orders table (name, product, quantity, pickup day, received day, status, note). state.days is the Days table: a day with a helper has limit: 12, otherwise the limit is 8. Booked hours are always added up from the Accepted orders, so they can never get out of step with the orders. state.inbox holds today's fetched messages, and state.log holds past changes and cancellations.
  • Back end (between BACK END and END BACK END): fetchToday, evaluate (checks every message in arrival order), rejectRequest, bookHelper, acceptAllAndNextDay, money. These never touch the page.
  • Front end: draws the page from state after every click, following the wireframe: today on the left, the next six days on the right with helper buttons, then changes and cancellations, money, and a list of all orders.
  • Messages: the seven days of Instagram messages (from InstagramOrders_Monday.html to InstagramOrders_Sunday.html) are stored in the app, so Fetch works without a server.
